Thesis

Sell the outcome. Own no labs. Keep the record.

  • What we sell

    Preclinical development as an outcome. A biotech brings a drug candidate and gets back the planned studies, the analysed results and the nonclinical package for its IND, instead of managing several labs by email.

  • How we deliver it

    A core that holds the study lifecycle, the audit trail and the human sign-offs, plus study drivers that each run one study type end to end with published methods.

  • Why it compounds

    We coordinate lab capacity and sell the outcome. Every programme adds structured, comparable data across labs to one record, an asset that grows with each programme.
