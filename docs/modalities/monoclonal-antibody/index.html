Before first-in-human

StudyGuidelineGLPStatus
Anti-drug antibody assessment in toxicity studies
Why

ADA must be measured to interpret exposure and findings; animal immunogenicity does not predict human immunogenicity

ICH S6(R1) section 3.6; Addendum section 4GLPRequired
Pharmacologically relevant species selection
Why

Toxicity studies must use species in which the product is pharmacologically active, established by target homology, binding and functional activity

ICH S6(R1) section 3.3; Addendum section 2Required
Human tissue cross-reactivity (IHC panel)
Why

Recommended for monoclonal antibodies before first human dosing

ICH S6(R1) section 3.3GLPRequired
Repeat-dose toxicity in relevant speciesSpecies: cynomolgus monkey
Why

S6(R1) section 4.4: duration follows the intended clinical use — generally 1 to 3 months, with 6 months supporting chronic use. S6(R1) gives no threshold that fixes one of those, so the duration is set by the programme rather than derived here (one relevant species identified: a single-species programme is acceptable)

ICH S6(R1) section 4.4; Addendum section 3.2GLPRequired
Standard genotoxicity battery
Why

The standard battery is not applicable to biotechnology-derived pharmaceuticals

ICH S6(R1) section 4.7GLPNot required
Safety pharmacology endpoints (within tox studies)
Why

Safety pharmacology endpoints may be incorporated into toxicity studies

ICH S6(R1) section 4.1GLPRequired

StudyGuidelineGLPStatus

Why
