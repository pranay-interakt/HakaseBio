Plasma protein binding across speciesSpecies: human, rat, dog

ICH M3(R2) section 3Required
Toxicokinetics within repeat-dose studies
Why

Systemic exposure must be measured in toxicity studies to relate findings to exposure; reported with the toxicity study (ICH M4S(R2) 4.2.3.2, 'including supportive toxicokinetics evaluations')

ICH S3AGLPRequired

Why

Mechanism and pharmacologically active dose are needed to set the starting dose and to interpret toxicity

The core battery must be completed before first administration in humans; it covers the central nervous (2.7.1), cardiovascular (2.7.2) and respiratory (2.7.3) systems

StudyGuidelineGLPStatus
Immunotoxicity weight-of-evidence review
Why

ICH S8 sections 2.1, 2.2Required
In vivo pharmacokinetics (ADME) in test speciesSpecies: rat, dog
Why

Further information on pharmacokinetics (absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion) in test species should be available before exposing large numbers of human subjects or treating for long duration (generally before Phase III); exposure in the tox species before first-in-human is covered by toxicokinetics (ICH S3A)

ICH M3(R2) section 3Required
Metabolite profiling and human metabolite comparison
Why

Human metabolites at >10% of total drug-related exposure that are disproportionate to animals require nonclinical characterisation

ICH M3(R2) section 3Required
Photosafety assessmentWaiting on: UV-visible absorption spectrum
Why

Triggered if the molecule has a molar extinction coefficient greater than 1000 L/mol/cm at any wavelength between 290 and 700 nm

ICH S10 sections 2, 5.1.1Conditional
Embryo-fetal development (rat and rabbit)Species: rat, rabbit
Why

Embryo-fetal assessment is required before inclusion of women of childbearing potential beyond the limited exposure M3(R2) permits (regional differences apply: EU and Japan expect it before any WOCBP exposure)

ICH M3(R2) section 11.3; S5(R3)GLPRequired
Fertility and early embryonic developmentSpecies: rat
Why

Male reproductive organs are evaluated in repeat-dose studies; a dedicated fertility study is expected before large-scale trials

ICH M3(R2) sections 11.1, 11.3GLPRequired

Before the marketing application

StudyGuidelineGLPStatus
Carcinogenicity assessment (2-year rat or S1B(R1) weight of evidence + rasH2 mouse)
Why

S1A: carcinogenicity studies are performed for any pharmaceutical whose expected clinical use is continuous for at least 6 months; M3(R2) places them before the marketing application; under S1B(R1) a weight-of-evidence assessment may replace the 2-year rat study

Pre- and postnatal developmentSpecies: rat
Why

Pre/postnatal development is submitted for marketing approval
