Hakase AI builds software for drug development where every number may end up in front of a regulator. BioSim is our product for the preclinical stage.

How we work

Clear purpose, traceable foundations, stated limits.

  • Science guides the engineering

    Each workflow starts from the question it supports and the evidence needed to answer it responsibly.

  • Disciplines work to one standard

    Scientific context, system design and day-to-day lab realities are considered together throughout development.

  • Limits are stated as clearly as capabilities

    When evidence is incomplete or a task is out of scope, we say so, and keep improving the work.
