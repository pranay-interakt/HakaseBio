Product

From candidate to IND, in four steps.

BioSim is the software your team uses to plan, run and document the preclinical studies that come before a first human trial. It keeps one record from the first plan to the IND.

Inside BioSim

What it is made of.

  • Study plan Every study your drug needs

    Lists the studies the ICH, FDA and WHO guidelines require for your drug, each linked to the section that asks for it. A missing fact marks a study as conditional; it is never assumed.

    More
  • Study order Find the deal-breakers early

    Puts the studies most likely to stop the programme first, weighing each one’s cost and time, within the order the guidelines allow.

    More
  • Study value Know which experiments are worth it

    Ranks studies by how much their result could change your go or no-go decision, and by what each one costs.

    More
  • Virtual lab Run every study in silico first

    Predicts the outcome of each required study with computer models and says how far each prediction can be trusted. Predictions guide the programme; signed studies make the submission.

    More
  • Lab studies The same standard at every lab

    14 study types, each with its protocol, data checks, analysis and report built in, so every contract lab is held to the same standard.

    More
  • Audit trail Every number shows where it came from

    Each value is labelled as measured, from the literature, calculated or predicted. Nothing is guessed, and every change is logged.

    More
  • Package check Reviewers can check it without us

    Every exported package can re-run its own analyses from the raw files and confirm nothing was changed, without BioSim or an internet connection.

    More
  • Rules for AI AI that stays accountable

    Hakase’s six rules for AI in regulated work: every answer traceable, its confidence stated, and people make every safety decision.

    More

What BioSim is not

Clear lines, so you know what you are buying.

  • Not a wet lab

    BioSim runs no animal or bench work. Its virtual lab predicts results; the studies that count run at the contract labs and in-house teams you already use.

  • Not a notebook

    An electronic notebook records what happened. BioSim plans the work, checks what comes back and builds the submission from it.

  • Not a replacement for your experts

    Toxicologists, study directors and regulatory leads make and sign the decisions. BioSim gives them everything they need to do it.
