Inside BioSim.

Seven capabilities we built in-house, and the discipline they are built to. Together they are what makes a BioSim package traceable, defensible and fast to produce.

Regulatory engine

The study plan, derived and cited.

Reads a programme’s facts and derives every study the ICH, FDA and WHO guidelines require, for nine drug types, each line cited to its section. An unknown fact leaves a study conditional, never assumed.

Where it works in BioSim

KESTREL

Kill-early study sequencing.

Orders the study battery so a programme-ending finding surfaces at the lowest expected spend: each study’s chance of a decisive result, from cited detection rates, against its cost and time, inside the order the guidelines set.

Where it works in BioSim

SEXTANT

The value of an experiment.

Prices each candidate study by how much it could change the go or no-go decision, using the programme’s own costs of a wrong go and a wrong stop. Experiments are ranked by value, and by value per unit cost.

Where it works in BioSim

Digital study engine

In silico answers, graded for credibility.

Answers each required study with the most qualified computational method available and grades the credibility of that answer for its context of use under ASME V&V 40. A prediction informs a decision; it never replaces a signed study.

Where it works in BioSim

Study drivers

One study type, end to end.

Fourteen study types, from microsomal stability to GLP repeat-dose toxicology, each packaged with its protocol, data checks, cited analysis and report, so every lab is held to the same standard.

Where it works in BioSim

Provenance ledger

Every value with its origin.

Eight ways a number can exist, none of them guessed. An append-only, hash-chained record of every change, with signatures only people can give, and simulated data that can never become evidence.

Where it works in BioSim

Offline verifier

The package checks itself.

Every Module 4 export carries what is needed to re-run each analysis from the raw files and check every fingerprint, ledger entry and signature, without BioSim, a database or a network.

Where it works in BioSim

HAIOps

Healthcare AI Operations: the discipline BioSim is built to.

Machine-learning operations was built for systems where a wrong answer costs a click. HAIOps is Hakase’s operating discipline for systems where a wrong answer reaches a patient. Six requirements, and how BioSim meets each.

RequirementIn BioSim
Traceability and audit readinessAn append-only ledger records inputs by fingerprint, method and version, reference-parameter version, software versions, actor and time. Corrections create a linked new version with a reason.
Quantified confidenceEvery in silico answer is graded under ASME V&V 40 against the risk of its context of use, and uncertainty travels with the value.
Human authority over safety decisionsThe signature boundaries of nonclinical development are first-class: a named person attests, and the system records exactly what was signed.
Applicability monitoringEach predicted value carries its applicability-domain check; each read-across names its analogues, their values and their similarity.
Lifecycle and model governanceA checksum-locked method registry. Every stored result names the method version that produced it, and a method change is a versioned event.
Integrity of evidencePredicted and simulated values are kept out of the evidence path by design. A study run on simulated data can be rehearsed and reported, and never approved.
