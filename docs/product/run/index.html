Product · Step 3

Run: any lab, one standard.

BioSim sends each study to the lab you choose, checks what comes back and analyses it with published methods. The Study Director and QA sign the result.

Dispatch

A complete package for the lab.

For each study, BioSim prepares a dispatch package: the protocol your team approved, the sample manifest, a data template, return instructions and a checksum manifest so nothing changes in transit. It works with an external contract lab, your own lab, or both.

Intake

Returned data is checked before anything is computed.

Results are validated against the protocol line by line. Every problem is reported with its line number, and rejected uploads are recorded too. A file that was simulated cannot be re-uploaded as measured.

Analysis

Study types BioSim runs end to end.

Each study type is a driver: protocol, data checks, analysis with cited methods, and report. Expert judgements such as a NOAEL are recorded as the Study Director’s signed decision.

Study typeWhat the analysis does
Bacterial reverse mutation test (Ames, OECD TG 471)Revertant counts by strain, metabolic activation and concentration against the concurrent and historical controls; the call is the Study Director's.
Bioanalytical method validation (chromatographic, ICH M10)Full validation of a chromatographic bioanalytical method against ICH M10 section 3: calibration, accuracy and precision, selectivity, matrix effect, carry-over, dilution integrity and stability.
Dose range-finding / MTD studyShort parallel-group study for dose selection: group comparisons, clinical-sign and mortality incidence, M3(R2) 1.5 high-dose facts; MTD and recommended doses by the Study Director.
In vitro potency and selectivity (4PL)Concentration-response potency at the primary target and off-targets by 4PL, with reference-compound checks and relative potency.
In vivo QT telemetry (ICH S7B; S7A cardiovascular)Conscious telemetry crossover: QT corrected by a justified method, change from baseline and from vehicle with CIs, exposure coverage; QTc and cardiovascular conclusions by the Study Director.
Liver microsomal metabolic stability (substrate depletion) with IVIVEIn vitro half-life by parent depletion in liver microsomes across species; CLint,app and well-stirred predicted hepatic clearance.
Peptide stability in blood, serum or plasmaDepletion of intact peptide in stated blood, serum or plasma conditions; half-life with 95% CI by the protocol's model, censoring beyond the last sample, and a plateau check for incomplete degradation.
Plasma protein binding by equilibrium dialysisFraction unbound in plasma across species by equilibrium dialysis, with the ICH M12 positive-control rule and the fu < 1% reliability flag.
Repeat-dose toxicity with Dunnett comparison to controlGroup design checked against OECD TG 407/408/409 and ICH M3(R2); per-endpoint Dunnett comparisons; NOAEL set by the Study Director.
Safety pharmacology: central nervous system (ICH S7A 2.7.1)Functional observation battery / modified Irwin covering the five S7A 2.7.1 domains; incidence by dose, sex and time; adverse CNS effect by the Study Director.
Safety pharmacology: respiratory system (ICH S7A 2.7.3)Respiratory rate with tidal volume and/or oxygen saturation, minute volume derived, each dose compared with vehicle at each timepoint; adverse effect by the Study Director.
Single-dose pharmacokinetics in a test species (NCA)Single-dose PK by non-compartmental analysis: Cmax, AUC, terminal phase selected by the scientist, t1/2, AUCinf, CL, Vz, Vss and absolute bioavailability.
Toxicokinetics in a toxicity study (ICH S3A)Systemic exposure per dose, sex and day within a toxicity study: non-compartmental Cmax and AUC, dose proportionality by the power model, observed exposure ratios between days, and incurred-sample reanalysis.
hERG (IKr) patch clamp concentration-response with safety marginConcentration-response of hERG current inhibition per ICH E14/S7B Q&A 2.1; IC50 with CI, Hill slope, and free-exposure margin per S7B Q&A 1.2.
