Product · Step 4

Submit: the nonclinical IND package, traced to raw data.

BioSim assembles the nonclinical part of your IND from the signed record, so every figure in it can be traced to the raw data behind it.

What you get

Built from the record, not retyped.

  • IND readiness

    A live view of which required studies are covered, in progress or missing, against the plan for your clinical protocol.

  • Module 2 summaries

    Drafts of the nonclinical overview (2.4) and summaries (2.6). Every number in the text is sourced, and a named author signs.

  • Module 4

    The nonclinical study reports assembled in the structure ICH M4S(R2) sets out.

  • SEND datasets

    Export for repeat-dose toxicity and toxicokinetic studies to SENDIG v3.1.1 with controlled terminology, Define-XML 2.1 and SAS XPORT v5, with checks run before export.

  • Offline verification

    Every Module 4 export can be re-checked without BioSim: each analysis is re-run from the raw files, and every file fingerprint, ledger entry and signature is verified.
