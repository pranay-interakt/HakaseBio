Glossary

The language of IND-enabling work.

Short definitions of the terms used across BioSim and preclinical drug development.

  • 21 CFR Part 11The FDA regulation on electronic records and electronic signatures.
  • ALCOA+Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, plus Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available. Data-integrity principles applied by FDA/MHRA to GxP records. In BioSim the ledger is the "original" record.
  • Applicability domainthe region of input space where a predictive model is reliable. Every PREDICTED quantity carries an ApplicabilityCheck; out-of-domain predictions are flagged.
  • Audit trail (ledger)BioSim’s append-only, hash-chained record of every change, which is the original record under ALCOA+.
  • CLhhepatic blood clearance, here from the well-stirred model.
  • Clinical holdAn FDA order to delay a proposed clinical investigation or suspend an ongoing one.
  • CLintintrinsic clearance. CLint,app (µL/min/mg microsomal protein) is the in vitro value; CLint,in vivo (mL/min/kg) is scaled to the whole liver.
  • CROcontract research organisation; an external laboratory that executes a study.
  • CTD / Module 4Common Technical Document (ICH M4); Module 4 holds the nonclinical study reports, numbered 4.2.1 (pharmacology), 4.2.2 (pharmacokinetics), 4.2.3 (toxicology).
  • Dunnett's testmultiple comparison of several treatment groups against one control (Dunnett 1955).
  • Extraction ratio (Eh)CLh / Qh; the fraction of drug removed in one pass through the liver.
  • fu,b / fu,punbound fraction in blood / plasma.
  • fu,mic / fu,incunbound fraction of drug in the microsomal incubation.
  • GLPGood Laboratory Practice, 21 CFR Part 58 (US) / OECD Principles of GLP.
  • HEDhuman equivalent dose, from an animal NOAEL by body-surface-area scaling (FDA 2005).
  • hERG / IKrthe potassium channel (KCNH2) carrying the rapid delayed rectifier current; inhibition can delay ventricular repolarisation (QT prolongation).
  • ICHThe International Council for Harmonisation of Technical Requirements for Pharmaceuticals for Human Use, whose guidelines (such as M3(R2) and S6(R1)) set the nonclinical studies regulators expect.
  • INDInvestigational New Drug application (21 CFR 312).
  • IND-enabling studiesThe nonclinical studies a sponsor completes before the first human dose and submits in its Investigational New Drug application.
  • IVIVEin vitro–in vivo extrapolation.
  • Km factorbody weight ÷ body surface area for a species (FDA 2005 Table 1).
  • MABELminimal anticipated biological effect level (EMA FIH guideline).
  • MPPGLmicrosomal protein per gram liver (mg/g).
  • MRSDmaximum recommended starting dose for first-in-human trials (FDA 2005).
  • NAMsNew approach methodologies: in vitro, in silico and other methods that can reduce or replace animal studies.
  • NOAELno-observed-adverse-effect level. In BioSim a NOAEL is a **human determination** recorded with a Study Director signature; the system reports statistics but never decides it.
  • PADpharmacologically active dose.
  • ProvenanceWhere a value came from: measured, literature, computed, predicted, guideline, user-supplied, simulated or unavailable.
  • Qhhepatic blood flow (mL/min/kg).
  • Safety pharmacologyStudies of a drug’s effects on vital functions: the central nervous, cardiovascular and respiratory systems.
  • SENDCDISC Standard for Exchange of Nonclinical Data.
  • Simulated datadata generated by a declared, cited model with recorded parameters and seed. Used for software verification and demonstration; never admissible as evidence.
  • Study Directorthe individual with overall responsibility for a GLP study (21 CFR 58.33).
  • Study setthe list of nonclinical studies a programme needs, derived from ICH rules and the programme context.
  • ToxicokineticsThe measurement of systemic exposure in a toxicity study, used to relate findings to exposure.
  • WOCBPwomen of childbearing potential.
