Science

Every result can be checked.

A number in a submission is only as good as its origin. BioSim records where every value came from, and refuses to produce one it cannot support.

Where values come from

Every value carries its origin.

There are eight. A guess is not one of them.

  • MeasuredFrom a study you ran, linked to its raw file.
  • LiteratureFrom a cited publication or database, with the record it came from.
  • ComputedCalculated by a named, versioned and cited method.
  • PredictedFrom a named, versioned model, only inside its stated applicability domain.
  • GuidelineTaken from a regulation or guidance, with the section.
  • User-suppliedEntered and attested by a named person.
  • SimulatedGenerated for rehearsal. Never evidence, never approvable.
  • UnavailableNot known, with the reason. Never replaced by a default.

Principles

How the record stays reliable.

  • Nothing is guessed

    If a value cannot be obtained, BioSim returns it as unavailable with the reason: a database that did not answer, a study not yet run, a fact not yet decided.

  • Simulated stays simulated

    Results derived from simulated data stay marked as simulated, cannot be approved and never enter Module 4.

  • Same inputs, same answer

    Before dispatch and before reporting, BioSim re-runs the analysis and refuses to continue if it no longer reproduces the recorded result.

  • People decide

    A NOAEL, a call on an Ames test, a choice of species: these are signed determinations by the person who owns them, never outputs of the software.

  • Methods are published

    Every calculation names its method, version, assumptions, limits and references. The registry is open below.
