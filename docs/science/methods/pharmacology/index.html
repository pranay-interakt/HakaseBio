Methods registry

Pharmacology methods.

4 methods in this area. Each lists the assumptions it makes and the limits outside which its result is not valid.

pharm.hill_4pl · version 1.0.0

Four-parameter logistic (Hill) concentration-response fit

Assumptions

  • a single sigmoidal transition between two asymptotes

Limits

  • an inflection point outside the tested concentration range yields an extrapolated potency which is flagged rather than reported as measured

References

  • DeLean A, Munson PJ, Rodbard D. Simultaneous analysis of families of sigmoidal curves: application to bioassay, radioligand assay, and physiological dose-response curves. Am J Physiol. 1978;235(2):E97-E102. doi.org/10.1152/ajpendo.1978.235.2.E97

pharm.molar_to_mass_concentration · version 1.0.0

Molar to mass concentration (IC50 in ng/mL)

Assumptions

  • molecular weight of the registered structure (free form, not a salt)

Limits

None stated.

References

  • ICH E14/S7B Implementation Working Group. Clinical and Nonclinical Evaluation of QT/QTc Interval Prolongation and Proarrhythmic Potential: Questions and Answers. Adopted 21 February 2022. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E14-S7B_QAs_Step4_2022_0221.pdf

pharm.percent_inhibition · version 1.0.0

Per-cell fractional current inhibition with background subtraction

Assumptions

  • residual current after a saturating selective blocker is non-hERG background (E14/S7B Q&A 2.1 item 2)
  • drug effect measured at steady state

Limits

  • rundown during the recording is not corrected; vehicle controls show its magnitude

References

  • ICH E14/S7B Implementation Working Group. Clinical and Nonclinical Evaluation of QT/QTc Interval Prolongation and Proarrhythmic Potential: Questions and Answers. Adopted 21 February 2022. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E14-S7B_QAs_Step4_2022_0221.pdf
  • Ridder BJ, Leishman DJ, Bridgland-Taylor M, et al. A systematic strategy for estimating hERG block potency and its implications in a new cardiac safety paradigm. Toxicol Appl Pharmacol. 2020;394:114961. doi.org/10.1016/j.taap.2020.114961

pharm.relative_potency · version 1.0.0

Relative potency: ratio of fitted 50% effective concentrations (off-target / primary)

Assumptions

  • the two curves are fitted independently; the standard error is the first-order delta method for a ratio of independent estimates

Limits

  • the ratio holds at the 50% effect level; with non-parallel curves it changes with the effect level (Ritz et al. 2015)
  • IC50s from assays at different substrate or ligand concentrations are not affinities; no Ki conversion is applied

References

  • Ritz C, Baty F, Streibig JC, Gerhard D. Dose-response analysis using R. PLoS One. 2015;10(12):e0146021. doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0146021
