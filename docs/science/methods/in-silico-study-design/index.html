In silico study design methods.

2 methods in this area. Each lists the assumptions it makes and the limits outside which its result is not valid.

insilico.design_recommendation · version 1.0.0

Structural change indicated by a failing predicted property

Assumptions

  • a tactic is proposed only where the cited literature ties it to the specific property that failed

Limits

  • the direction of the expected change is given; the magnitude is not predicted and must come from re-measuring or re-computing the modified structure
  • a tactic is a hypothesis for a chemist to test, never an instruction

insilico.virtual_repeat_dose · version 1.0.0

Projected repeat-dose study: exposure per group and the findings it would provoke

Assumptions

  • a group reports an adverse finding when its projected exposure reaches the endpoint's critical exposure interval
  • exposure at each group is predicted by the mechanistic model, not interpolated from a dose-normalised constant

Limits

  • a projection of what the study would report; it is not the study and cannot stand in its place in a regulatory submission
  • an endpoint a species cannot physiologically express is excluded, not projected
  • every projected NOAEL is interval-censored by the dose spacing of the design
