2 methods in this area. Each lists the assumptions it makes and the limits outside which its result is not valid.

seq.average_mass · version 1.0.0

Average molecular mass of a polypeptide chain: the sum of the average residue masses plus one water (ExPASy Compute pI/Mw)

Assumptions

  • one linear chain with free termini

Limits

  • post-translational modifications, disulfide bonds and glycans are not included
  • ambiguity codes (B, Z) and X have no defined mass, so no mass is computed
  • the table's selenocysteine average uses an older selenium atomic weight (about 0.011 Da low per residue against the 2024 value)

References

  • Gasteiger E, Hoogland C, Gattiker A, Duvaud S, Wilkins MR, Appel RD, Bairoch A. Protein identification and analysis tools on the ExPASy server. In: Walker JM, ed. The Proteomics Protocols Handbook. Humana Press; 2005:571-607. doi.org/10.1385/1-59259-890-0:571
  • SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, ExPASy. Protein Identification Tools - Mass values used (FindMod): average and monoisotopic amino-acid residue masses and water. web.expasy.org/findmod/findmod_masses.html
