Methods registry

Toxicology methods.

2 methods in this area. Each lists the assumptions it makes and the limits outside which its result is not valid.

tox.exposure_margin_m3r2 · version 1.0.0

Exposure margin: animal group-mean exposure at the top dose / clinical exposure

Assumptions

  • 'exposure' generally means group mean AUC; Cmax may be more appropriate for acute cardiovascular or CNS effects (M3(R2) Note 1)

Limits

  • both exposures are inputs with their own provenance

References

  • ICH Harmonised Tripartite Guideline M3(R2): Guidance on Nonclinical Safety Studies for the Conduct of Human Clinical Trials and Marketing Authorization for Pharmaceuticals. Step 4, 11 June 2009. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/M3_R2__Guideline.pdf

tox.incidence · version 1.0.0

Per-group counts of animals at start, unscheduled deaths and animals showing each clinical sign

Assumptions

  • tabulated as OECD TG 407 paragraph 50 describes for each test group

Limits

None stated.

References

  • OECD. Test No. 407: Repeated Dose 28-day Oral Toxicity Study in Rodents. OECD Guidelines for the Testing of Chemicals, Section 4. Adopted 2008. doi.org/10.1787/9789264070684-en
