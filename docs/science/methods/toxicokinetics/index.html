None stated.

tk.dose_proportionality_power · version 1.0.0

Power-model slope with a confidence interval against a pre-stated acceptance interval

Assumptions

  • PK = beta0 * Dose^beta1, fitted on ln-ln scale by least squares over individual animals (parallel groups)
  • the acceptance limits theta_L/theta_H and confidence level are stated before analysis

Limits

  • control animals and unquantifiable exposure are excluded (log of zero)
  • the interval form is the exact algebraic consequence of the power model; Smith 2000 is cited from its abstract

References

  • Smith BP, Vandenhende FR, DeSante KA, Farid NA, Welch PA, Callaghan JT, Forgue ST. Confidence interval criteria for assessment of dose proportionality. Pharm Res. 2000;17(10):1278-1283. doi.org/10.1023/A:1026451721686
  • Hummel J, McKendrick S, Brindley C, French R. Exploratory assessment of dose proportionality: review of current approaches and proposal for a practical criterion. Pharm Stat. 2009;8(1):38-49. doi.org/10.1002/pst.326
  • Braeckman R, Guenther S, Mickle TC, Barrett AC, Smith A, Oh C. Dose proportionality and steady-state pharmacokinetics of serdexmethylphenidate/dexmethylphenidate, a novel prodrug combination to treat attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder. J Child Adolesc Psychopharmacol. 2022;32(5):288-295. doi.org/10.1089/cap.2022.0015
  • Shang H, Dai X, Li M, Kai Y, Liu Z, Wang M, Li Q, Gu Y, Liu C, Si D. Absolute bioavailability, dose proportionality, and tissue distribution of rotundic acid in rats based on validated LC-QqQ-MS/MS method. J Pharm Anal. 2022;12(2):278-286. doi.org/10.1016/j.jpha.2021.03.008

tk.exposure_multiple · version 1.0.0

Animal exposure divided by the stated human exposure

Assumptions

  • the human exposure is an input with its own provenance (measured or predicted) and the same matrix and analyte

Limits

  • total, not unbound, exposure unless both inputs are unbound (ICH S3A section 2)

References

  • ICH Harmonised Tripartite Guideline S3A: Note for Guidance on Toxicokinetics: The Assessment of Systemic Exposure in Toxicity Studies. Step 4, 27 October 1994. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/S3A_Guideline.pdf

tk.exposure_ratio_days · version 1.0.0

Observed ratio of group mean exposure on a later profile day to the first

Assumptions

None stated.

Limits

  • an observed ratio; it is an accumulation ratio at steady state only if steady state is established, which is a judgement (CDISC ARAUC)

References

  • CDISC SDTM Pharmacokinetic Parameters controlled terminology (PKPARM/PKPARMCD), published in the NCI Thesaurus, version 26.09d (2026-09-28). evsexplore.semantics.cancer.gov/evsexplore/concept/ncit/C85565
  • ICH Harmonised Tripartite Guideline S3A: Note for Guidance on Toxicokinetics: The Assessment of Systemic Exposure in Toxicity Studies. Step 4, 27 October 1994. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/S3A_Guideline.pdf

tk.group_summary · version 1.0.0

Group mean, SD, median and range of individual exposure parameters

Assumptions

  • descriptive statistics per dose, sex and day (ICH S3A 3.9)

Limits

  • small groups give low precision; individual data may be more informative (ICH S3A 3.9)

References

  • ICH Harmonised Tripartite Guideline S3A: Note for Guidance on Toxicokinetics: The Assessment of Systemic Exposure in Toxicity Studies. Step 4, 27 October 1994. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/S3A_Guideline.pdf
