Methods registry

Safety pharmacology methods.

4 methods in this area. Each lists the assumptions it makes and the limits outside which its result is not valid.

safety.free_cmax_qa12 · version 1.0.0

Free high clinical exposure with the 1% unbound-fraction floor

Assumptions

  • fu,p below 1% is set to 1% because of protein-binding measurement uncertainty (S7B Q&A 1.2)

Limits

  • the clinical Cmax,ss is an input; its uncertainty is not propagated

References

  • ICH E14/S7B Implementation Working Group. Clinical and Nonclinical Evaluation of QT/QTc Interval Prolongation and Proarrhythmic Potential: Questions and Answers. Adopted 21 February 2022. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E14-S7B_QAs_Step4_2022_0221.pdf

safety.herg_margin · version 1.0.0

hERG safety margin over free therapeutic exposure

Assumptions

None stated.

Limits

  • a single-channel in vitro margin; it is not a proarrhythmia prediction

References

  • Redfern WS, Carlsson L, Davis AS, et al. Relationships between preclinical cardiac electrophysiology, clinical QT interval prolongation and torsade de pointes for a broad range of drugs. Cardiovasc Res. 2003;58(1):32-45. doi.org/10.1016/S0008-6363(02)00846-5
  • ICH Harmonised Tripartite Guideline S7B: The Nonclinical Evaluation of the Potential for Delayed Ventricular Repolarization (QT Interval Prolongation) by Human Pharmaceuticals. Step 4, 12 May 2005. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/S7B_Guideline.pdf
  • ICH E14/S7B Implementation Working Group. Clinical and Nonclinical Evaluation of QT/QTc Interval Prolongation and Proarrhythmic Potential: Questions and Answers. Adopted 21 February 2022. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E14-S7B_QAs_Step4_2022_0221.pdf

safety.minute_volume · version 1.0.0

Minute volume as tidal volume x respiratory rate, per animal and timepoint, summarised by group

Assumptions

  • tidal volume as reported by the acquisition system, with its own corrections

Limits

  • a derived measure; ICH S7A 2.7.3 names respiratory rate and, as examples, tidal volume or haemoglobin oxygen saturation

References

  • Lim R, Zavou MJ, Milton PL, et al. Measuring respiratory function in mice using unrestrained whole-body plethysmography. J Vis Exp. 2014;(90):e51755. (Secondary statement of minute volume = tidal volume x frequency.) doi.org/10.3791/51755

safety.observation_incidence · version 1.0.0

Animals with a finding (a score other than the protocol's normal score) per group and timepoint

Assumptions

  • dose-response and time course are described per S7A 2.4.1; the observation list and scoring are protocol inputs

Limits

  • descriptive: no statistical test for incidence is applied (none is verified for S7A core-battery endpoints)

References

  • ICH Harmonised Tripartite Guideline S7A: Safety Pharmacology Studies for Human Pharmaceuticals. Step 4, 8 November 2000. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/S7A_Guideline.pdf
