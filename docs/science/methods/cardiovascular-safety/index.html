Cardiovascular safety methods.

7 methods in this area. Each lists the assumptions it makes and the limits outside which its result is not valid.

cv.change_from_baseline · version 1.0.0

Change from baseline and from vehicle (within-animal, crossover): mean, 95% CI and p-value by Student's t

Assumptions

  • each animal receives every treatment (Latin-square crossover); differences are approximately normal

Limits

  • one test per timepoint; no multiplicity adjustment

References

  • ICH E14/S7B Implementation Working Group. Clinical and Nonclinical Evaluation of QT/QTc Interval Prolongation and Proarrhythmic Potential: Questions and Answers. Adopted 21 February 2022. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E14-S7B_QAs_Step4_2022_0221.pdf
  • Student. The probable error of a mean. Biometrika. 1908;6(1):1-25. doi.org/10.2307/2331554

cv.exposure_multiple · version 1.0.0

Assumptions

Limits

None stated.

References

cv.qtc_bazett · version 1.0.0

QTc by Bazett: QT / RR^(1/2), RR in seconds

Assumptions

  • formula as stated in Vandenberk 2016; Bazett's 1920 paper could not be read and the corrected-QT form is attributed to Taran and Szilagyi 1947 (Cobos Gil 2008)

Limits

  • a fixed rate-correction coefficient (S7B Q&A 3.3)

References

  • Vandenberk B, Vandael E, Robyns T, et al. Which QT correction formulae to use for QT monitoring? J Am Heart Assoc. 2016;5(6):e003264. (Readable statement of the Bazett and Fridericia formulae.) doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.116.003264
  • Cobos Gil MA, Garcia Rubira JC. Who was the creator of Bazett's formula? Rev Esp Cardiol (Engl Ed). 2008;61(8):896-897. (Attribution caveat: the corrected-QT form is due to Taran and Szilagyi 1947.) doi.org/10.1016/S1885-5857(08)60243-1
  • ICH Harmonised Tripartite Guideline S7B: The Nonclinical Evaluation of the Potential for Delayed Ventricular Repolarization (QT Interval Prolongation) by Human Pharmaceuticals. Step 4, 12 May 2005. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/S7B_Guideline.pdf

cv.qtc_fridericia · version 1.0.0

QTc by Fridericia: QT / RR^(1/3), RR in seconds

Assumptions

None stated.

Limits

  • a fixed rate-correction coefficient; S7B Q&A 3.3 prefers individual correction when the drug affects heart rate

References

  • Fridericia LS. Die Systolendauer im Elektrokardiogramm bei normalen Menschen und bei Herzkranken. Acta Med Scand. 1920;53(1):469-486. English translation: Ann Noninvasive Electrocardiol. 2003;8(4):343-351, doi:10.1046/j.1542-474X.2003.08413.x. doi.org/10.1111/j.0954-6820.1920.tb18266.x
  • Vandenberk B, Vandael E, Robyns T, et al. Which QT correction formulae to use for QT monitoring? J Am Heart Assoc. 2016;5(6):e003264. (Readable statement of the Bazett and Fridericia formulae.) doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.116.003264
  • ICH Harmonised Tripartite Guideline S7B: The Nonclinical Evaluation of the Potential for Delayed Ventricular Repolarization (QT Interval Prolongation) by Human Pharmaceuticals. Step 4, 12 May 2005. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/S7B_Guideline.pdf

cv.qtc_individual · version 1.0.0

Individual-animal QT correction: QT - b_i (RR - RR_ref), b_i the slope of each animal's QT on RR fitted on its vehicle-treatment data

Assumptions

  • a linear QT-RR relationship over the observed RR range in each animal
  • the reference RR is stated in the protocol (published implementations differ)

Limits

None stated.

References

  • ICH E14/S7B Implementation Working Group. Clinical and Nonclinical Evaluation of QT/QTc Interval Prolongation and Proarrhythmic Potential: Questions and Answers. Adopted 21 February 2022. database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E14-S7B_QAs_Step4_2022_0221.pdf
  • Chui RW, Baublits J, Chandra FA, et al. Evaluation of moxifloxacin in canine and non-human primate telemetry assays. Clin Transl Sci. 2021;14(6):2379-2390. (Published individual-animal QT correction.) doi.org/10.1111/cts.13103
  • Engwall MJ, Baublits J, Chandra FA, et al. Evaluation of levocetirizine in beagle dog and cynomolgus monkey telemetry assays. Clin Transl Sci. 2023;16(3):436-446. (Published individual-animal QT correction.) doi.org/10.1111/cts.13454

QTc-RR independence: least-squares slope with 95% CI, correlation and p-value
