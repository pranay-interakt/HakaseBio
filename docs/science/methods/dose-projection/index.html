Dose projection methods.

3 methods in this area. Each lists the assumptions it makes and the limits outside which its result is not valid.

dose.hed_body_weight · version 1.0.0

Human equivalent dose by body weight: the animal dose in mg/kg, unconverted

Assumptions

  • FDA 2005 section V.C: proteins administered intravascularly with Mr above 100,000 daltons are normalised to mg/kg rather than by surface area

Limits

  • applies to the categories the guidance names; for a small molecule given systemically the surface-area conversion is the one that applies
  • the guidance also allows mg/kg where the NOAEL occurs at a similar mg/kg dose across species, which is a judgement about the data and is not made here

References

  • U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Guidance for Industry: Estimating the Maximum Safe Starting Dose in Initial Clinical Trials for Therapeutics in Adult Healthy Volunteers. CDER, July 2005. www.fda.gov/media/72309/download

dose.hed_bsa · version 1.1.0

Human equivalent dose by body-surface-area scaling

Assumptions

  • systemic exposure scales with body surface area across species

Limits

  • not applicable where the FDA guidance directs mg/kg or exposure-based scaling, nor to biologics dosed on a MABEL basis
  • FDA 2005 section V.C excludes this conversion for three categories: a dose limited by local toxicity on an alternative route, a product given into an anatomical compartment it does not leave, and a protein above 100,000 daltons given intravascularly

References

  • U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Guidance for Industry: Estimating the Maximum Safe Starting Dose in Initial Clinical Trials for Therapeutics in Adult Healthy Volunteers. CDER, July 2005. www.fda.gov/media/72309/download

dose.mrsd · version 1.0.0

Maximum recommended starting dose from the most sensitive species

Assumptions

  • the NOAEL-derived HED of the most sensitive relevant species governs

Limits

  • must be compared against the pharmacologically active dose and, for immune agonists and biologics, against MABEL; the lowest value governs

References

  • U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Guidance for Industry: Estimating the Maximum Safe Starting Dose in Initial Clinical Trials for Therapeutics in Adult Healthy Volunteers. CDER, July 2005. www.fda.gov/media/72309/download
  • EMA. Guideline on strategies to identify and mitigate risks for first-in-human and early clinical trials with investigational medicinal products. EMEA/CHMP/SWP/28367/07 Rev. 1, July 2017. www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/scientific-guideline/guideline-strategies-identify-and-mitigate-risks-first-human-and-early-clinical-trials-investigational-medicinal-products-revision-1_en.pdf
