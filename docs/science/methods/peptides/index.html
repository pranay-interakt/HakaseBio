Peptides methods.

3 methods in this area. Each lists the assumptions it makes and the limits outside which its result is not valid.

peptide.first_order_decay · version 1.0.0

First-order depletion of intact peptide: ln-linear regression of percent remaining on time; k = -slope, t1/2 = ln(2)/k, 95% interval from the slope's t-interval (n - 2 degrees of freedom), transformed monotonically

Assumptions

  • complete first-order loss (plateau zero)
  • replicates pooled after normalising each to its own time zero

Limits

  • a half-life longer than the last sampling time is an extrapolation and is not reported (censored)
  • incomplete degradation biases k; checked with the one-phase decay model

References

  • Obach RS. Prediction of human clearance of twenty-nine drugs from hepatic microsomal intrinsic clearance data: an examination of in vitro half-life approach and nonspecific binding to microsomes. Drug Metab Dispos. 1999;27(11):1350-1359. doi.org/10.1016/S0090-9556(24)14938-0
  • Cavaco M, Valle J, Flores I, Andreu D, Castanho MARB. Estimating peptide half-life in serum from tunable, sequence-related physicochemical properties. Clin Transl Sci. 2021;14(4):1349-1358. doi.org/10.1111/cts.12985
  • Student. The probable error of a mean. Biometrika. 1908;6(1):1-25. doi.org/10.2307/2331554

peptide.one_phase_decay · version 1.0.0

One-phase exponential decay with a plateau, Y = (Y0 - Plateau) exp(-K t) + Plateau, by nonlinear least squares; t1/2 = ln(2)/K; 95% intervals from the asymptotic covariance with the t distribution on n - 3 degrees of freedom

Assumptions

  • independent, equally weighted residuals on the percent scale

Limits

  • needs at least four observations; the intervals are asymptotic (Wald)
  • a plateau above zero means part of the peptide is not degraded and the half-life describes only the degrading part

References

  • GraphPad Software. Equation: One phase decay. GraphPad Prism 11 Curve Fitting Guide (vendor documentation; equation form and half-life = ln(2)/K). www.graphpad.com/guides/prism/latest/curve-fitting/reg_exponential_decay_1phase.htm
  • Cavaco M, Valle J, Flores I, Andreu D, Castanho MARB. Estimating peptide half-life in serum from tunable, sequence-related physicochemical properties. Clin Transl Sci. 2021;14(4):1349-1358. doi.org/10.1111/cts.12985
  • Student. The probable error of a mean. Biometrika. 1908;6(1):1-25. doi.org/10.2307/2331554

peptide.percent_of_time_zero · version 1.0.0

Intact peptide remaining as a percentage of the time-zero sample of the same replicate

Assumptions

  • the parent response is proportional to the amount of intact peptide

Limits

  • a sample below the quantitation limit is excluded, not imputed
