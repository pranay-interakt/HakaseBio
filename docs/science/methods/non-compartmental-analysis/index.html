nca.auc_last · version 1.0.0

AUC from time zero to the last measurable concentration (trapezoidal rule as stated)

Assumptions

  • actual sampling times (ICH M13A 2.2.2.2)
  • BLQ at or before the first quantifiable concentration = 0; later BLQ excluded

Limits

  • no extrapolation beyond the last measurable concentration
  • a profile without a time-zero sample has no AUC(0-t)

nca.cmax_tmax · version 1.0.0

Maximum observed concentration and its time

Assumptions

  • taken directly from the data without interpolation (FDA 2022 III.A.9.a); the first occurrence is used when the maximum is tied

Limits

  • with a monitoring design (1-3 samples per interval) the observed maximum estimates Cmax only roughly (ICH S3A Note 1)

nca.lambda_z · version 1.0.0

Terminal rate constant by least squares of ln C on t over the selected terminal points

Assumptions

  • the terminal phase is selected by the scientist (start time); at least three declining points (FDA 2022 Appendix A; ICH M13A 2.1.8)

Limits

  • the vendor 'best fit' (largest adjusted R-squared) is shown as a suggestion only
