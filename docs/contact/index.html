What happens in a demo?
Forty minutes on a candidate you choose. We derive its study plan, show the suggested order and trace any result back to its source.
Do you need our data before a demo?
No. A drug type, route, indication and planned trial length are enough to start. Confidential data is only needed for a readiness review or a programme.
Which labs do you work with?
Any. BioSim prepares a dispatch package each lab can work from, and checks what comes back.
How is BioSim priced?
Per drug programme. A readiness review is priced per study reviewed. We quote after a first conversation.
