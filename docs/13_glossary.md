# 13 — Glossary

**ALCOA+** — Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, plus Complete, Consistent,
Enduring, Available. Data-integrity principles applied by FDA/MHRA to GxP records. In HOS the
ledger is the "original" record (`docs/10`).

**Applicability domain** — the region of input space where a predictive model is reliable. Every
`PREDICTED` quantity carries an `ApplicabilityCheck`; out-of-domain predictions are flagged.

**Attestation** — a human electronic signature with legal meaning. Only human principals holding
the role can sign (`hos/kernel/attestation.py`).

**CLint** — intrinsic clearance. `CLint,app` (µL/min/mg microsomal protein) is the in vitro value;
`CLint,in vivo` (mL/min/kg) is scaled to the whole liver.

**CLh** — hepatic blood clearance, here from the well-stirred model.

**Conformance kit** — the shared test battery every study driver must pass (`docs/08`).

**CRO** — contract research organisation; an external laboratory that executes a study.

**CTD / Module 4** — Common Technical Document (ICH M4); Module 4 holds the nonclinical study
reports, numbered 4.2.1 (pharmacology), 4.2.2 (pharmacokinetics), 4.2.3 (toxicology).

**Driver** — a package implementing `StudyDriver` for one study type: design, plan, data template,
instruction package, ingest, analysis, interpretation, report and optional simulation.

**Dunnett's test** — multiple comparison of several treatment groups against one control
(Dunnett 1955).

**Execution target** — where a study's work physically runs: an external laboratory, an in-house
laboratory, or a simulation.

**Extraction ratio (Eh)** — CLh / Qh; the fraction of drug removed in one pass through the liver.

**fu,mic / fu,inc** — unbound fraction of drug in the microsomal incubation.

**fu,b / fu,p** — unbound fraction in blood / plasma.

**GLP** — Good Laboratory Practice, 21 CFR Part 58 (US) / OECD Principles of GLP.

**GxP plane / discovery plane** — the regulated and non-regulated halves of the system (`docs/10`).

**HED** — human equivalent dose, from an animal NOAEL by body-surface-area scaling (FDA 2005).

**hERG / IKr** — the potassium channel (KCNH2) carrying the rapid delayed rectifier current;
inhibition can delay ventricular repolarisation (QT prolongation).

**IND** — Investigational New Drug application (21 CFR 312).

**IVIVE** — in vitro–in vivo extrapolation.

**Km factor** — body weight ÷ body surface area for a species (FDA 2005 Table 1).

**Ledger** — the append-only hash-chained record of every state change (`hos/kernel/ledger.py`).

**MABEL** — minimal anticipated biological effect level (EMA FIH guideline).

**MPPGL** — microsomal protein per gram liver (mg/g).

**MRSD** — maximum recommended starting dose for first-in-human trials (FDA 2005).

**NOAEL** — no-observed-adverse-effect level. In HOS a NOAEL is a **human determination** recorded
with a Study Director signature; the system reports statistics but never decides it.

**PAD** — pharmacologically active dose.

**Provenance kind** — one of measured, literature, computed, predicted, guideline, user-supplied,
simulated, unavailable (`docs/07` §7.2).

**Qh** — hepatic blood flow (mL/min/kg).

**Quantity** — the provenance-typed value object; the only form in which a scientific number exists.

**SEND** — CDISC Standard for Exchange of Nonclinical Data.

**Simulated data** — data generated by a declared, cited model with recorded parameters and seed.
Used for software verification and demonstration; never admissible as evidence.

**Study set** — the list of nonclinical studies a programme needs, derived from ICH rules and the
programme context (`hos/core/regulatory`).

**Study Director** — the individual with overall responsibility for a GLP study (21 CFR 58.33).

**UNAVAILABLE** — the honest representation of a missing value: no number, a stated reason.

**WOCBP** — women of childbearing potential.
