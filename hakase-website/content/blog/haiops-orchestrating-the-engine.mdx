When we describe HakaseAI to scientists, we say "one engine." When we describe
it to platform engineers, we say "HAIOps."

HAIOps is the operations layer underneath the modules. It does four jobs:

1. **Orchestration.** Routes work across 35 specialist agents organized into 4
   swarms, plus 4 BioDigital Twin curators.
2. **Gates.** Enforces the falsifier registry — no prediction ships without a
   confirmation slot.
3. **Governance.** Tenant isolation, privacy-first defaults, never trains on
   customer data.
4. **Audit.** Signed, versioned, exportable evidence packs for V&V40 / MID3.

## Why it lives outside the modules

If gates lived inside each module, every product team would re-invent the same
plumbing. By promoting gates and governance to a shared control plane, every
module inherits the same evidence discipline. That's how a discovery prediction
can travel cleanly into a clinical protocol and into a payer dossier without
losing provenance.

## What you see as a user

HAIOps mostly stays out of the way. You see it when a prediction comes with a
falsifier card attached, when an evidence pack auto-assembles for submission,
or when a confirmation result quietly updates the priors behind your next run.
